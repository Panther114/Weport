screenshots are retained outside the repository in the local final-audit folder.

the registered path and single uninstall entry, compare installed EXE/ASAR/host
hashes against the fresh payload, and preserve protected profile hashes.

in subsequent formats. The later cold-profile matrix and repeat/corruption check

do not prove all source media are available or all cold-cache behavior is covered.

external connector delivery and every reporter-specific case remain unverified.
